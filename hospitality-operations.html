Housekeeping & Maintenance Management

One clear system for room cleaning, inspections, maintenance requests, priorities and completed work.

HousekeepingMaintenanceAdmin DashboardPWAReports

Business value

Designed to give hotels and accommodation teams a reliable live overview while reducing calls, paper lists and missed tasks.

Problem

Cleaning and maintenance teams often coordinate through calls, paper lists and scattered messages, making priorities and room status difficult to follow.

Solution

A mobile-first operations system that connects rooms, employees, tasks, faults, photos and live status in one daily workflow.

Features

  • Room cleaning schedules and status
  • Maintenance requests with photos and priority
  • Tasks assigned to responsible employees
  • Inspections, notes and completion history
  • Live overview for management
  • Reports for rooms, work and response times

Technology

Responsive Progressive Web App, mobile-first task interface, role-based views, structured records, photo documentation and reports.

Result

Teams know what must be done, who is responsible and which rooms are ready, while management gets a clear record of daily operations.
