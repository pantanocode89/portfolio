Group Menu Ordering System

A complete digital ordering system for restaurant groups, including QR access, participant ordering, administration, kitchen lists and PDF reports.

PWAFirebaseAdmin DashboardQR OrderingPDF

Business value

Designed to make group ordering clearer, reduce manual work and give staff faster access to order and kitchen information.

Problem

Restaurant group orders can become difficult to manage when multiple participants, menu choices, notes and kitchen lists are handled manually.

Solution

A QR-based ordering flow with an admin dashboard, order overview, kitchen preparation lists and PDF output for daily operations.

Features

  • QR access for group participants
  • Participant ordering workflow
  • Administration dashboard
  • Kitchen lists and PDF reports
  • WhatsApp integration

Technology

Progressive Web App, Firebase, Firestore, responsive UI, PDF generation and admin tooling.

Result

The business receives a clearer ordering process, less manual coordination and a digital system that supports real restaurant workflows.
