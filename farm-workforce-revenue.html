Agricultural Management System

Farm Workforce & Revenue Management

A flexible application for managing agricultural workers, daily wages, tasks, expenses, income and seasonal reports.

AgricultureWorker ManagementRevenue TrackingPWAReports

Business value

Designed for farms and seasonal work where workers, daily wages, machine costs, transport and revenue need to stay organized.

Problem

Seasonal agricultural work often depends on paper notes, manual wage calculation and scattered records for workers, crops, tasks and expenses.

Solution

A flexible PWA that can adapt to fruit picking, vineyards, vegetable production, machine work, fuel costs, transport and different seasons.

Features

  • Worker and attendance management
  • Daily wage and task tracking
  • Income and expense calculation
  • Individual worker reports
  • PDF and mobile reports
  • Support for different crops and activities

Technology

Progressive Web App, mobile-first interface, reports, structured data and installable app experience.

Result

The farm gets a clearer overview of workers, costs, income and seasonal results, with reports that can be used on phone or computer.
